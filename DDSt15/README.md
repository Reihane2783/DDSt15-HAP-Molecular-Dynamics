# DDSt15 Molecular Dynamics

Molecular dynamics simulation and analysis of the DDSt15 peptide in aqueous solution using GROMACS.

The DDSt15 structure was independently prepared for this study, and the molecular dynamics simulations and subsequent analyses were performed using GROMACS.

The design of DDSt15 and the methodological framework were based on the following study:

Ferrari, C. R., Netz, P. A., Kitamoto, K. S. de A., et al. (2026).
*A novel statherin-derived phosphomimetic peptide (DDSt15) presents affinity for hydroxyapatite and effectively protects against intrinsic dental erosion.*
Dental Materials, 42, 440–450.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2025.11.007
